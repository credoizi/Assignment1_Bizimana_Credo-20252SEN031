## 1. Business Scenario

This project is centered around a simple online shopping system. This system is used to support the entire process of online shopping. The database used for this system stores four types of core information: relevant materials of all registered customers, detailed content of commodities available for sale on the platform, all order records submitted by customers, and the details of which specific commodities were purchased in each order.

1.1. Main Tables

 -customers — stores Customer Information

 -products — stores commodity information and prices

 -orders — stores customer orders

 -order_items — stores the commodities included in each order and the corresponding purchase quantity

## 🔗 2. JOIN Queries

The JOIN queries are used to combine and retrieve information from different tables in the database.

### 1️⃣ Order Query That Associates Customer Information

This query combines order information with the corresponding customer information. It displays the order ID, customer name, city, and order date. An INNER JOIN is used to connect the orders and customers tables.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business know which customer placed each order, the customer's city, and when the order was placed.

### 2️⃣ Order Items and Product Information

This query combines order item information with product information. It displays the product name, category, price, and quantity purchased.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business understand which products are being purchased and the quantity of each product.

### 3️⃣ All Customers and Their Orders

This query uses a LEFT JOIN to display all customers and their orders, including customers who have not placed any orders.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business identify customers who have placed orders as well as customers who have not yet placed an order.

## 🧩 3. CTE Query

### Customer Spending Above Average

The CTE query calculates the total amount spent by each customer and then identifies customers whose total spending is above the average spending.

*Result:*

* John Smith — 3100.00
* Alice Brown — 1830.00

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business identify customers who spend more than the average and understand customer spending behavior.

---

## 4. Window Function Queries

### 1️⃣ Rank Customers by Total Amount Spent

This query ranks customers based on their total spending, starting with the customers who spent the highest amount.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business compare customer spending levels and identify customers with higher total purchases.

### 2️⃣ Number Each Customer's Orders

This query numbers each customer's orders according to the date they were placed.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business understand the order sequence for each customer, such as their first, second, or later order.

### 3️⃣ Running Total of Revenue

This query calculates the revenue generated by each order and the running total of revenue over time.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business track how total revenue increases as new orders are placed.

### 4️⃣ Days Between Customer Orders

This query calculates the number of days between a customer's current order and their previous order.

*Business interpretation:*
This helps the business understand how frequently customers return to make another purchase.
